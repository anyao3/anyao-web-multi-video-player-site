FIRST LAUNCH ON MAC

One extra step for an unsigned build.

If macOS says the app is damaged, move it to Applications, open Terminal, run the command below, then launch it again.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Anyao Web Multi Video Player.app"

Only run this for a package you intentionally downloaded from this official page. Windows SmartScreen may similarly require “More info” → “Run anyway” for an unsigned build.

FAQ

Know before you download.

Does the app download or copy videos?

No. It organizes page URLs and plays media supplied by supported third-party services. You remain responsible for complying with each service’s terms and applicable law.

What is imported from my browser?

The import reads bookmark titles and URLs. It does not import passwords, browsing history, or authentication cookies.

Why is a supported video sometimes unavailable?

A video may require login, be region-restricted, be removed, block embedding, or change its player implementation. Compatibility with services other than those marked Recommended is provided on a best-effort basis.

Can every video play simultaneously?

Not guaranteed. Service restrictions, network speed, video resolution, and your computer’s CPU/GPU and memory determine the practical limit.

How does the Pro license work?

It is a one-time lifetime license for up to five active devices. Deactivate an old device before moving the license when possible. License validation uses Polar and includes a limited offline grace period.

Where is my data stored?

Bookmarks, playlists, settings, and local license state are stored on your device. Opening pages and playing media necessarily sends requests to the selected website and its media providers.
